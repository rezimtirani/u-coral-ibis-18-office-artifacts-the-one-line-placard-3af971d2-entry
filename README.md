One line from a museum label: a lanyard from a modern office, treated as an ancient artifact dug from a lost civilisation — a ritual cord of passage whose plastic talisman was never read, only flashed at doors, and the doors always believed it.

> Lanyard, woven nylon, c. 2011. Ritual cord of the Open-Plan Tribe; its talisman was never read, only flashed at doors, and the doors always believed it. Gift of Human Resources.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3 standard library only) writes the placard to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`, checked first against the competition gate: 1 to 180 characters of UTF-8 text, exactly one line, with its catalogue date, material and donor all present.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
